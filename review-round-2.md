
A note on innovation and originality

During our initial scan, our AI noticed that your plugin falls into a very common category: ✨ LLMs.txt Generation.

There are currently over 150 plugins in the directory that already address this specific area. While we welcome all contributions, we highly encourage authors to consider how their plugin will stand out in such a crowded category.

We recommend searching the directory to see what is already published.

    Consider the impact you want to have. If your plugin provides similar functionality to dozens of others, it may struggle to find an audience, which can be frustrating for some developers. Before moving forward, think about whether you'd prefer to continue with this approach or pivot to a new, innovative idea that hasn't been done yet.
    Consider the audience. A specialized tool for a particular feature or a highly specific workflow often has a much greater impact than a general one. By narrowing your focus, you can become the "go-to" solution for a dedicated group of users rather than getting lost in a crowded, general category.
    Look for the "missing link" Think about where the WordPress ecosystem is heading. Is there a gap in modern core features (like the Site Editor), a new API integration, or a connection between existing tools that hasn't been built? Creating something that fills a genuine void provides immediate, visible value and usually gains traction much faster than a replica of existing tools.
    Consider the power of collaboration. Sometimes the most effective way to have an impact is to join forces. Contributing your ideas or features to an established plugin can improve the experience for thousands of existing users immediately. This often achieves more for the community than starting from scratch.


Managing expectations:

When a plugin enters a saturated category without a distinct feature, it can be difficult to gain traction. We often hear from authors concerned that their plugin isn't appearing in search results or gaining users — We already checked, search is fine — In a crowded category, users tend to choose established options unless a new plugin offers a truly fresh and innovative alternative.

WordPress thrives on fresh ideas. We encourage you to look at the directory not just for what is there, but for what is missing. A unique approach often leads to much higher user adoption and provides much more value to the ecosystem and to your own growth as a developer.

Please be aware that our manual review process evaluates the uniqueness and utility of each submission. If this plugin is found to be too similar to existing ones without offering significant new value or a distinct approach, we may choose to reject this submission.

We look forward to seeing how you can push the boundaries of what a WordPress plugin can do.
